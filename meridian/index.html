Settings

Intelligence Engine (LLM)

Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly to the provider you choose. It is never committed to the repository or sent anywhere else.

When enabled, the model performs live open-source research so the brief reflects current reporting rather than only training data.

Recipient Profile

Shapes the Impact to My Work and What I Should Know Today sections. Strategic/role level only — do not enter sensitive internal, program, or network details.

Email Delivery (EmailJS)

Static sites cannot send mail directly. MERIDIAN uses EmailJS, a client-side service. Create an EmailJS account + template, then paste the IDs below. Your template should expose {{subject}}, {{to_email}} and {{html}} (or {{message}}) variables.
